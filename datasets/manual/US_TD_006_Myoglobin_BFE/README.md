myoglobin, in OpenAUC format, published by its creators on Zenodo under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license.

## Changes from the source

`experimental_data.zip` is the source file, unchanged (8,764,275 bytes, md5
`a2e218d755dc5920e89c42e0a38c8e3d`). The record's other file, the spectral
spreadsheet `Myoglobin Spec Data 15SEP25.xlsx`, is not included; it is
available from the source.

## Contents

One run, `MartinR_Dobler-BFE-Run-7_15SEP25-run2424`: 50 `RI` files, cells 1,
3, 4, 5 and 7, channels A and B, at 220, 230, 280, 400 and 410 nm, with the
run's experiment, profile and time-state files.

## Use here

Real multi-wavelength `RI` data from a band-forming experiment, a layout the
synthetic corpus does not cover.

## Limitations

The analysis needs the band-forming solver described in the related
publication. Experimental conditions not in the OpenAUC files are in the
publication.
